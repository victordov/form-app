Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy explains how Form handles consumer health data, as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada Senate Bill 370 and similar state laws. It adds to our Privacy Policy, which describes all of Form’s data practices.

Form is published by Victor Dovgaliuc, an individual developer based in the United States (“we”, “us”). Contact: victordov@gmail.com.

1. Health data that stays on your iPhone

Most consumer health data in Form is kept on your iPhone, and we never receive it. This includes:

  • your treatment plans, doses, check-ins, symptoms, notes, measurements, lab results and attached report files, supplies, appointments and questions
  • data Form reads from Apple Health with your permission
  • images you take with the camera when you scan a lab report
  • reports and backup files Form creates

This data is not sent to us or to any third party. It leaves your phone only if you choose to share a report or save a backup file somewhere else, or through your own iCloud Backup or computer backup.

2. Health data we or our processors may receive

Apart from anything you choose to tell us by email, the only consumer health data that we or our processors may receive is:

  • If you turn analytics on: the fact that you use a medication and health tracker, and how you use its features. This comes from product events and simple properties, such as “dose recorded” or “check-in saved”, linked to a random ID. It never includes medicine or peptide names, doses, symptoms, notes, measurements, lab values or Apple Health data. The full list is in the Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase information: what you bought in Form and related App Store transaction details, as described in the Privacy Policy.

If you email us, we receive what you send, which may include health information if you choose to include it. We use it only to reply.

3. Sources

  • You: what you enter in Form, and what you send us by email.
  • Your device: app usage events if analytics is on, device and app details, and purchase information from the App Store.
  • Apple Health: only with your permission. This data stays on your iPhone.

4. Purposes

  • On your iPhone: to show your records, schedule the reminders you set, and create the reports you ask for.
  • Analytics, if you turn it on: to understand which features are used, so we can fix problems and improve Form.
  • Purchase information: to confirm and restore what you have bought.
  • Email: to reply to you.

5. Who receives it

The only recipients are these processors, which act for us under contract:

Recipient Categories of data
PostHog, Inc. Analytics events and properties, the random ID, and device and app details. Only if analytics is on.
RevenueCat, Inc. Purchase and subscription information. The analytics ID too, only if analytics is on.

We don’t share consumer health data with any other third party or affiliate.

When you save data to Apple Health, share a report or save a backup file elsewhere, you are directing that yourself, to a place you choose.

6. No sale

We do not sell consumer health data.

7. Consent

  • We collect consumer health data beyond what’s needed to provide the features you use only with your consent. Usage analytics is off unless you turn it on.
  • Giving data to a processor so it can provide a service for us is not “sharing” under these laws. Beyond that, we share consumer health data only with your consent. Today we don’t share it with anyone.
  • You can withdraw consent to analytics at any time in Account › Privacy.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data
  • access that data
  • get a list of the third parties we have shared it with
  • have it deleted
  • withdraw your consent
  • appeal if we refuse your request

Data on your iPhone is under your control. You can view, export and delete it yourself in Form. Account › Delete all data on this device removes your records from the phone.

To appeal a refusal, reply to our response with the word “Appeal”. If you live in Washington and your appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General.

9. How to exercise your rights

Email victordov@gmail.com and tell us what you are asking for. We may need some details to verify your request. Most data in Form never leaves your phone, so we may hold nothing about you.

We will respond within 45 days of receiving your request. If more time is reasonably necessary, we may extend this once by another 45 days. If we do, we’ll tell you within the first 45 days.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page with a new date, and tell you in the app or on this page.

Questions: victordov@gmail.com

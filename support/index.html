Getting started

  • Form runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. There’s no account and no sign-in.
  • Add the treatment your clinician prescribed, with the dose and schedule exactly as prescribed. Form doesn’t recommend, calculate or adjust doses.
  • Log each dose as taken or skipped. Use the daily check-in for mood, energy (1 to 10), symptoms and notes.
  • Setup asks about reminders, Apple Health and usage analytics. Each is optional, and you can change it later in Account.

Backups and moving to a new iPhone

Your records are stored only on your iPhone. Form doesn’t sync them with iCloud, so keep a backup.

  • Form keeps automatic daily backups on your iPhone, the last seven, in Files › On My iPhone › Form › Backups. These stay on the same phone.
  • To keep a copy somewhere else, go to Account › Backup & restore › Back up now, then save the file to iCloud Drive or send it with AirDrop.
  • On the new iPhone, install Form. Then open the .formbackup file, or tap Restore a backup on the welcome screen.
  • Restoring replaces the records on that phone. Form saves a copy of the current records first.
  • If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is turned on for your iPhone, it also includes Form’s data.

A backup file contains your records. Once you save it somewhere else, keep it somewhere you trust.

Apple Health

  • Connect Apple Health in Health & labs. iOS asks which data you want to allow.
  • With your permission, Form reads weight, body fat, waist circumference, sleep, steps, dietary protein and water. It saves the weight, waist and body fat you log in Form.
  • To change permissions, open the Health app, then Profile › Apps › Form.
  • Data Form saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Reminders

  • Allow notifications when Form asks. If you said no earlier, turn them on in Settings › Notifications › Form.
  • You choose the reminders: doses, refills, appointments and a daily check-in. They’re scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Medicine names are hidden on the lock screen. To show or hide the name for a plan, use the setting in the plan editor.

Subscriptions

  • Tracking features are free. Form Pro adds a weekly review, change review, 90-day trends and the full PDF report.
  • To manage or cancel, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period or trial to avoid renewal.
  • If you bought Form Pro and it isn’t showing, tap Restore purchases in Account or on the Form Pro screen.
  • Apple handles payments and refunds. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Prices and billing details are in the Terms of Use.

Usage analytics

  • Usage analytics is off unless you turn it on. Change it any time in Account › Privacy › Share usage analytics.
  • Turning it off stops collection and resets the random ID.
  • It never includes medicine or peptide names, doses, symptoms, notes, measurements, lab values or anything from Apple Health.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

  • Account › Delete all data on this device removes your records, attached lab files and the backup files Form keeps on the phone. It also cancels reminders and resets analytics.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from the phone.
  • Backup files you saved elsewhere, and data saved to Apple Health, need to be deleted there.
  • Deleting data doesn’t cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • To ask about data we may hold, such as your support emails, email us. See your rights.

Medical questions

Form doesn’t recommend doses or give medical advice. Ask your clinician about doses, side effects, lab results or changes to your treatment.

In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · Last updated October 21, 2026

Summary

  • Your records are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • Apple Health data is used only inside Form and in reports you create. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Usage analytics is off unless you turn it on. Even then, it never includes medicine names, doses, symptoms, notes, measurements, lab values or Apple Health data.
  • Apple processes payments. RevenueCat confirms what you’ve bought.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites. We don’t sell personal data.
  • You can delete everything on your phone in Account › Delete all data on this device.

This summary is not the whole policy. The details are below.

1. Who we are

Form is published by Victor Dovgaliuc, an individual developer based in the United States (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: victordov@gmail.com

This policy explains how the Form app for iPhone handles personal data. If you live in Washington, Nevada or another state with a consumer health data law, also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

2. Your records on your iPhone

Your records are what you enter in Form: treatment plans, doses, check-ins, symptoms, notes, measurements, lab results and attached report files, supplies, appointments and questions.

  • They are stored only on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection.
  • They are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Form does not sync them to iCloud.
  • If you have turned on iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, those backups include Form’s data. These are Apple’s services, covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Because we never receive your records, we can’t see, change, recover or delete them for you. You control them in the app.

3. Backups

  • Form keeps automatic daily backup files on your iPhone, the last seven, in Files › On My iPhone › Form › Backups.
  • Back up now creates a backup file and lets you choose where to save it, for example iCloud Drive, AirDrop or another app. Once you save it elsewhere, that copy is under your control, and the terms of the place you saved it apply.
  • Restoring a backup replaces the records on your phone. Form saves a copy of your current records first.

4. Apple Health

Form uses Apple Health (HealthKit) only if you give permission.

What Form reads
Weight, body fat, waist circumference, sleep, steps, dietary protein and water.
What Form writes
The weight, waist circumference and body fat you log in Form.

Health data is used only to show it in Form and in reports you create. It is:

  • never sent to us or to third parties
  • never used for advertising, marketing or data mining
  • never sold
  • never stored in iCloud by Form

You can change permissions in the Health app. Data Form saved to Apple Health stays in Apple Health until you delete it there.

5. Camera, notifications and reports

Camera
Form uses the camera only when you scan a lab report. The image stays on your device.
Notifications
Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Medicine names are hidden on the lock screen unless you choose to show them for a plan.
Reports and summaries
Form creates them on your iPhone. They are shared only when you choose to share them, using the share sheet. Form never sends reports on your behalf.

6. Usage analytics (optional)

Usage analytics helps us understand which features are used, so we can fix problems and improve Form. It is provided by PostHog, Inc., with servers in the United States.

Your choice

  • It is off by default.
  • It is on only if you choose Share usage analytics during setup, or turn it on in Account.
  • You can turn it off any time in Account › Privacy. This stops collection and resets the random ID.

What Form sends when analytics is on

  • Names of product events, for example onboarding completed, plan created, dose recorded, check-in saved, report exported, paywall viewed, purchase started or completed, and reminder opened.
  • Simple properties, for example counts, schedule type, units system, membership tier, where a purchase started, and the number of days in a period you chose.
  • A random ID created on your device.
  • App version, device model, iOS version, and language and region settings.

What Form never sends

Medicine or peptide names, doses, symptoms, notes, measurements, lab values, or anything from Apple Health.

IP addresses and identification

PostHog receives your device’s IP address as part of the internet connection. Form’s PostHog project is set to discard IP addresses rather than store them.

The ID is random and isn’t tied to your name or email, so we usually can’t single out your events. Analytics events are kept for no longer than 24 months. Analytics data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

7. Purchases

Apple processes payments. We never receive your payment card details.

RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) processes purchase and subscription information to confirm what you have bought:

  • App Store transaction and receipt data
  • a random app user ID
  • the product, price and currency
  • storefront country
  • app version, device model and iOS version

If analytics is on, Form also gives RevenueCat your analytics ID, so purchase events can be connected to app usage. Turning analytics off removes that link.

8. Support emails

If you email us, we receive what you send: your email address, your message and any attachments. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete it when it’s no longer needed. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes messages under Google’s privacy policy.

9. What we don’t do

  • Form has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Form shows no advertising.
  • Form doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It doesn’t use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and doesn’t ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t share it for targeted advertising.

10. Why we use data and our legal bases

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR or UK GDPR requires a legal basis for each use of personal data. Ours are:

Purpose Legal basis
Usage analytics, including linking purchase events to app usage Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Because using a medication and health tracker can reveal information about your health, also your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)).
Confirming and restoring your purchases Performance of a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b))
Replying to your support emails Our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f))
Keeping the app secure Our legitimate interest in protecting Form and its users (Art. 6(1)(f))

You can withdraw your consent to analytics at any time by turning it off. This doesn’t affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

Your records and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone and are handled by the app on your device. We don’t receive them.

11. Who receives data

PostHog, Inc.
Usage analytics, only if you turn it on. A service provider and processor acting for us.
RevenueCat, Inc.
Purchase and subscription information. A service provider and processor acting for us.
Apple
An independent provider of the App Store, iCloud and Apple Health. Apple’s handling of your data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

We don’t share personal data with anyone else, except where the law requires us to disclose it, for example to answer a valid court order.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses to run and secure the service, under GitHub’s privacy statement. The website has no analytics or cookies of its own.

12. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and PostHog and RevenueCat process data in the United States. If you are outside the United States, your data is transferred there when you use analytics, make a purchase or email us.

According to these providers, they rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom.

13. How long data is kept

  • Records on your iPhone: until you delete them or the app. We never hold them.
  • Automatic backups on your iPhone: the last seven daily files.
  • Analytics events: no longer than 24 months.
  • Purchase information: as long as it’s needed to confirm and restore your purchases, or longer if the law requires it.
  • Support emails: until they’re no longer needed to reply to you.

14. Security

Your records are protected by iOS Data Protection, which works when your iPhone has a passcode. We limit what leaves your device to what’s described in this policy. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify affected people and regulators as the law requires, for example under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.

15. Children

Form is not directed to children. It is intended for adults 18 and over. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

16. Deleting your data

  • Account › Delete all data on this device removes your records, attached lab files and the backup files Form keeps on the phone. It also cancels reminders and resets analytics.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from the phone.
  • Backup files you saved elsewhere, and data saved to Apple Health, must be deleted there.
  • Cancelling a subscription is separate. Apple handles it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

To ask us to delete support emails or other data we may hold, see How to make a request.

17. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • restrict how we use it
  • object to how we use it
  • receive it in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing before you withdrew it
  • complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example where you live or work

Your records are on your iPhone, so you can view, correct, export and delete them yourself in Form.

18. US state privacy rights

If you live in California, Washington, Nevada, Connecticut or another state with a privacy law, you may have the right to:

  • know about and access the personal data we hold about you
  • delete it
  • correct it
  • opt out of the sale of personal data and of targeted advertising. We do neither, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • appeal if we refuse your request. Reply to our response with the word “Appeal”.

We won’t treat you differently for using these rights. Washington and Nevada residents also have the rights in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

19. How to make a request

Email victordov@gmail.com and tell us what you are asking for. We may need some details to verify your request. We will respond within the time required by the law that applies to you.

Most data in Form never leaves your phone, so we may hold nothing about you. Because analytics uses a random ID, we usually can’t find your analytics events. Turning analytics off resets that ID.

20. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page with a new date, and tell you in the app or on this page.
